# Assignments are modified in place

A change to an existing assignment, such as a time change, reassignment, swap or shortening for an early departure, updates the same record rather than deleting it and creating a new one. The record keeps its identity and a history of previous values. That keeps the original assignment, every change to it and the resulting pay traceable to one another, which the audit log and payout traceability (FR-C5) depend on.

## Consequences

Re-saving a day from the side panel must update existing assignments where it can, and create or delete only where runs actually begin or end.
