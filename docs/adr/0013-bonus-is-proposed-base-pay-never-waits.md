# The bonus is a proposal the Administrator confirms; base pay never waits for it

The bonus engine calculates **bonus potential** and the Administrator confirms it at the weekly close. The system doesn't decide the bonus, because missing clock-ins, anomalies and disputed penalties may still be open when the week ends. Base pay comes from assigned hours, needs no judgement, and is released every Monday regardless. Only the bonus is withheld, and only for weeks with an open bonus hold. Once resolved, a bonus is paid as an itemised adjustment in the next open pay period. A closed period is never reopened.

## Consequences

- The close must be one list with every clean week preselected and a single confirm action. The Owner has one evening to close about 40 staff, and forty individual confirmations would recreate the manual bottleneck the system replaces.
- The weekly bonus and streak records can be recalculated: waiving a penalty re-evaluates that week and recalculates the streak forward, which can make a streak bonus payable for earlier weeks.
- A pay period can be force-closed with a logged reason, so it can never become permanently unclosable.
