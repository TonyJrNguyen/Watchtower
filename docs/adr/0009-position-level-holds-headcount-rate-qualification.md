# Headcount, rate and qualification live at the position level only

Seven positions (QC, Thu Ngân – Offline, Thu Ngân – Online, Pha chế, Phục vụ, Kiểm tra đơn, Bếp) carry staffing needs, default rates and qualifications. Their **sub-positions** and **attributes** (for example Pha chế - Matcha, Phục vụ - Bưng bàn) are optional, can be combined, and are descriptive only: they never change headcount, coverage, rate, pay, qualification flags or the bonus. Colour on the calendar identifies the position, never the sub-position. The client confirmed this on 2026-09-28.

## Consequences

- Thu Ngân – Online and Thu Ngân – Offline are two separate positions, not sub-positions of one cashier position, because each needs its own headcount and rate.
- Two sub-positions of the same position on one assignment don't make it a shared assignment.
- The client says "role" for a position. Code and docs say **position**, and "role" is reserved for access (Superadmin / Administrator / Staff).
