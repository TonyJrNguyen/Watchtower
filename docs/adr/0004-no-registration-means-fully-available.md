# No registration means fully available

A staff member who registers nothing for a week is treated as available for every period of every day. Registration exists to record constraints, so no registration means no constraints. Anyone who can't work a period must register explicitly, like everyone else.

## Consequences

The "forgot to register" versus "free all week" risk is handled in three ways:

- The Administrator sees the difference between an explicit all-periods registration and a staff member counted as free.
- Staff counted as free are never flagged as below the contract expectation.
- A reminder before the cutoff says that silence counts as full availability. This is on Zalo in Phase 1 and a notification from Phase 2.

Staff counted as free are the most likely to appear at the publish gate. That isn't a problem with them: they simply weren't given a shift.
