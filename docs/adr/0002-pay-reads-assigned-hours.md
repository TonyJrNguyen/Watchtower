# Pay and the 8-hour test read assigned hours, not clocked hours

Work time comes from the **assignment** on the calendar. Punctuality comes from the arrival time, recorded by hand now and from the time clock later. Neither source ever stands in for the other. Arriving early earns nothing, and lateness inside the grace period never shortens counted hours. Lateness reaches pay only through the separate deduction, and reaches the bonus only through the late-penalty flag.

## Consequences

- There is no check-out. The working day ends at the assigned end time.
- The assignment must stay accurate, so a departure of more than 30 minutes is recorded by shortening the assignment and giving the rest to a cover. A departure of 30 minutes or less is paid in full and leaves the assignment unchanged: an accepted overstatement of at most 30 minutes.
- An unrecorded no-show still counts as worked until the Administrator cancels or shortens the assignment. This interim limitation is accepted and ends when the Phase 3 time clock raises missing clock-ins.

## Considered options

- The bonus reading **clocked hours** (the old G33) was superseded. Four minutes of lateness would have quietly pushed a day under 8 hours.
