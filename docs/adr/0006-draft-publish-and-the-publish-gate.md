# Draft, publish once, and the publish gate

A week's assignments are **draft**, invisible to staff and enforced server-side, until the Administrator publishes the week explicitly. A draft assignment may have **no position yet**, because the Owner works in two steps: first place enough people in each period, then give positions. Publishing is blocked while any active staff member has no assignment and no **skip** (a reason from a fixed list), or while any assignment has no position. The week is published once. Later changes take effect immediately, and anything created after publication, such as a cover, a swap or a new assignment, is published when it's created and so must carry a position from the start.

## Consequences

- The gate is a coverage check, not a judgement on the staff member. The usual reason for an empty week is that someone was overlooked, and the cost of that is finding out on Monday morning.
- The placing-then-positions order is guidance only. The system enforces nothing but the gate.
