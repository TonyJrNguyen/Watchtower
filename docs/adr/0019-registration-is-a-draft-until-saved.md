# A registration is a draft until the staff member saves it

A staff member's changes to their registration stay a draft until they press Save, and the Administrator only ever sees the last saved version (FR-S20). Before v1.9 every change reached the Administrator at once, so a half-finished edit could be read as the staff member's answer. When the registration window closes, an unsaved draft is saved automatically so nothing entered is lost, and only the staff member is told they didn't press Save (FR-S5).

## Consequences

- Availability keeps two versions per staff member and week, draft and saved, and the saved one records whether it was saved by the staff member or at the cutoff.
- Every rule that reads registration — the no-registration default (FR-S19), the below-contract flag (FR-O22), the calendar — reads the saved version only.
- Custom-time edits (FR-S21) commit into the draft, and the page's Save is refused while one is unsaved.
